The problem

Most plans assume one gym, one floor, one unbroken week.

Yours is not that week. You fly. You have two kitchens and two living rooms. Some nights the floor is a hotel carpet you will not swing on.

The plan that only works at home A is not a plan. It is a streak you break every ten days.

What you get

Eight weeks. Three sessions a week. Twenty to thirty minutes.

Every session ships four ways:

  • Hotel one bell or a hotel dumbbell, small room, neighbours. Hotel kettlebell workout guide.
  • Home A your usual floor.
  • Home B the other house, same session, different constraints.
  • Quiet no swings. Thin floors, late nights, shared walls.

One-bell substitutions throughout. If the hotel only has a dumbbell, you still train.

You download a PDF (works on a plane). You tick sessions in a Notion tracker that asks which house you are in, not whether you were “good”.

Who it is for
